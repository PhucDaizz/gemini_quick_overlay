=== HUONG DAN SU DUNG TOOL HO TRO ===

BUOC 1: CHUAN BI
- Dam bao may tinh da cai dat Google Chrome.
- Giai nen thu muc tool ra o cung (vi du o D: hoac Desktop).

BUOC 2: KHOI DONG
- Chi can chay file "START_TOOL.bat".
- Phan mem se tu dong mo Chrome va chay ngam.
- Lan dau tien, Chrome se hien ra -> Ban can DANG NHAP vao https://gemini.google.com/app mot lan duy nhat.

BUOC 3: SU DUNG
1. Copy noi dung can hoi (Boi den + Ctrl C).
2. Nhan phim F2 de gui cau hoi.
3. Dap an se hien o goc trai duoi man hinh.
   - Nhan F4 de xem lai dap an cu.
   - Nhan ESC de tat dap an ngay lap tuc.

=== KHAC PHUC LOI ===
1. Loi "Khong tim thay Google Chrome":
   - Nguyen nhan: Do ban cai Chrome o vi tri khac (khong phai mac dinh).
   - Cach sua:
     + Chuot phai vao shortcut Chrome tren Desktop -> Chon Properties.
     + Copy dong "Target" (vi du: D:\MySoft\Chrome.exe).
     + Mo file "chrome_path.txt" trong thu muc tool.
     + Paste duong dan do vao roi Luu lai.
     + Chay lai START_TOOL.bat.

2. Loi khong hien dap an:
   - Thu kiem tra xem ban da dang nhap Gemini trong cua so Chrome do chua.
   - Thu tat het Chrome va chay lai tool.
